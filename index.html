About Our College

Ravindra College of Engineering for Women (RCEW) is an autonomous engineering institution located in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh. The college focuses on quality education, technical knowledge, practical learning, and student development.

Our goal is to encourage young women to become skilled engineers, innovators, and future leaders.
